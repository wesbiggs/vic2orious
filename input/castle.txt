# CASTLE MATHESON

King Matheson has ruled these lands for three decades, but only since Maldrath the dragon arrived has he suffered defeat. Two armies were sent to banish the dragon: only a few badly burnt survivors returned each time.

His hold on the kingdom is tenuous. Monsters have begun to roam the land, wreaking havoc and striking terror into the faces of all they meet.

He bides his time in the castle. But for how long?
