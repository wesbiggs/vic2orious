# PRINCESS ELEWYN

Fearing for her safety, the king locked his only daughter away in a tower, where her only visitors are the birds that come to her window each morning.

She has learned to speak to them, and they often sit on her windowsill, listening to her read tales of daring knights and their adventures.

She longs for freedom and the chance to experience more of the world, and to one day meet a real hero of the land.
