# THE GAOLER

The Gaoler once worked for the king, keeping thieves and cutthroats under lock and key in the dark depths below the castle.

But too many years of living without the sunlight, and the constant company of evildoers, drove him mad. In his delirium, he murdered every prisoner: man, woman, and child. He fled the castle's gates and wandered into the wilderness, and try as he might, he could not wash off the bloodstains on his hands.
